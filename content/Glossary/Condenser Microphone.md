[[Sound Waves|Sound waves]] move the [[Diaphragm|diaphragm]] that sits close to a [[Charged|charged]] [[Backplate|backplate]]. The vibrations change the distance, which change the [[Capacitance|capacitance]] between the diaphragm and the backplate. It requires power to operate. A condenser microphone is extremely sensitive and fragile, and is best for studio vocals and acoustic instruments. It is often used for podcasts or voiceovers.
