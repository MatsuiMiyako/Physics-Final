A sound wave is a vibration that travels through a medium (like air or water) before reaching your ears. Sound waves also push and pull the air around it, creating "pressure waves" that move outward. When the [[Particle|particles]] are squished together it is called a compression and when they are spread out they are called rarefactions. Your brain translates these vibrations in the air as sound. Sound waves are [[Longitudinal Wave|longitudinal waves]].
