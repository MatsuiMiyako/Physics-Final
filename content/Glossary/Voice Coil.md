A voice coil is a small coil of wire that is attached to the [[Diaphragm|diaphragm]] in a [[Dynamic Microphone|dynamic microphone]] capsule. It moves within the [[Magnetic Field|magnetic field]] created by a [[Magnet|magnet]] in the [[Microphone Capsule|capsule]] to generate a electrical signal through [[Electromagnetic Induction|electromagnetic induction]].
