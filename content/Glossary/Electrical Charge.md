Objects that have an electric charge will experience a [[Force|force]] when placed in an [[Electromagnetic|electromagnetic]] [[Field|field]]. An electric charge can be positive, negative or neutral. A positive charge typically has more [[Electrons|electrons]] than [[Protons|protons]]. A negative charge typically has fewer electrons than protons. A neutral charge has the same number of electrons and protons.
