A [[Electret|electret]] microphone is a type of [[Condenser Microphone|condenser microphone]] but it doesn't need a high-[[Voltage|voltage]] to operate it. This is because it has a electret in its [[Microphone Capsule|capsule]]. These are the small and cheap microphones found in phones, laptops, headsets, and clip-on microphones.
