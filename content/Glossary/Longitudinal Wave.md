A longitudinal wave is a type of wave where the [[Particle|particles]] move back and forth in the same direction the wave moves. Sound is a longitudinal wave because when you make a sound, the air particles move back and forth in place. These air particles bump into the air particles beside them, passing the sound along. This "pushing" is in the same direction the sound is moving, which makes sound a longitudinal wave.
