A microphone capsule is the part of the microphone that captures the sound and converts it into electrical signals. All capsules have a diaphragm that vibrates when sound waves hit it. Condenser microphones have a rigid metal plate behind the diaphragm called a backplate while dynamic mics have a voice coil and magnet instead of a backplate. Basically, a microphone capsule is the heart of the microphone.
