A microphone capsule is the part of the microphone that captures the sound and converts it into electrical signals. All capsules have a [[Diaphragm|diaphragm]] that vibrates when [[Sound Waves|sound waves]] hit it. [[Condenser Microphone|Condenser microphones]] have a rigid metal plate behind the diaphragm called a backplate while dynamic mics have a [[Voice Coil|voice coil]] and [[Magnet|magnet]] instead of a [[Backplate|backplate]]. Basically, a microphone capsule is the heart of the microphone.
