A backplate is a rigid metal plate behind the diaphragm of a condenser microphone capsule. Together with the diaphragm, it forms a capacitor whose capacitance changes as the diaphragm moves in response to sound waves. The backplate holds a fixed electrical charge to maintain the capacitance between the diaphragm and backplate and converts diaphragm movement into an electrical signal.
