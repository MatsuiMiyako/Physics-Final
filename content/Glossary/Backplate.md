A backplate is a rigid metal plate behind the [[Diaphragm|diaphragm]] of a [[Condenser Microphone|condenser microphone]] [[Microphone Capsule|capsule]]. Together with the diaphragm, it forms a [[Capacitor|capacitor]] whose [[Capacitance|capacitance]] changes as the diaphragm moves in response to [[Sound Waves|sound waves]]. The backplate holds a fixed [[Electrical Charge|electrical charge]] to maintain the capacitance between the diaphragm and backplate and converts diaphragm movement into an electrical signal.
