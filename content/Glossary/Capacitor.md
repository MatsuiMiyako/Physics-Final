A capacitor is an electrical component formed by two [[Conductor|conductive]] surfaces separated by an [[Insulator|insulating]] gap. In a [[Condenser Microphone|condenser microphone]] [[Microphone Capsule|capsule]], the [[Diaphragm|diaphragm]] and [[Backplate|backplate]] are the two conductive surfaces while the gap between them act as the insulating gap. Capacitors store a [[Electrical Charge|electrical charge]] that can be quickly released when needed.
